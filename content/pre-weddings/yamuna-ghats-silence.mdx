Captured over two mornings along the sacred banks of the Yamuna in Mathura, this visual story was conceived as a meditation on stillness, heritage, and natural light.

We avoided typical pre-wedding props and artificial sets, focusing instead on authentic couple interaction amidst ancient stone ghats and dawn river mist.
